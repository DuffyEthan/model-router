# Savings report, 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21

Approximate figures from one user's Claude Code usage. All costs are API-equivalent at list prices, not what a Pro or Max subscriber pays. See [README.md](README.md) for how to reproduce and the caveats.

## Coverage

- Date range: 2026-10-19 to 2026-10-21 (UTC, inclusive). The routing log has activity on 10-20 and 10-21 only.
- 13 sessions, 74 prompts (73 typed by the user), 394 API requests, about 50.1M tokens.
- 0 requests fell before a session's first routing-log record.
- The router was turned off for roughly a day (10-20 midday to 10-21 morning). During that stretch every request ran on the session model.

## Takeaways

- Routing saved about 10% against an Opus-only baseline ($2.69 of $27.47), but this window is dominated by time with the router off: 68% of spend came from unrouted Opus requests.
- Among routed prompts the picture is better. Sonnet and Haiku handled 62% of routed prompts (23 of 37) for about 12% of total spend ($2.88 of $24.78), though prompt count is not a measure of difficulty.
- Switching and classifying are cheap. The switch cache penalty was $0.98 (about 4% of actual cost) and the classifier cost half a cent over 37 calls.
- The simulated `sonnet-first` policy would have saved roughly 35% on the same requests, against 10% observed. Much of that gap is the router-off period, and the comparison says nothing about whether answers would have been as good.
- Sample is small (13 sessions over two active days, one override). Do not extrapolate from it.
