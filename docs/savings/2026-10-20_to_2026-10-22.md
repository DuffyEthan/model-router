# Savings report, 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-22

Approximate figures from one user's Claude Code usage. All costs are API-equivalent at list prices, not what a Pro or Max subscriber pays. See [README.md](README.md) for how to reproduce and the caveats.

## Headline

With the router on, the same work cost **29% less** than running it all on Opus 5.5: $13.73 against $19.38, across 10 sessions and 294 API requests. That figure already includes the cache re-writes caused by switching models and the classifier's cost.

## Coverage

- Date range: 2026-10-20 to 2026-10-22 (UTC), with 2026-10-22 covered up to about 11:40 UTC.
- 13 sessions, 90 prompts (84 typed by the user), 540 API requests, about 66.8M tokens.
- 0 requests fell before a session's first routing-log record.
- The router was off from midday on 10-20 to the morning of 10-21. Every request in that stretch ran on the session model, Opus 5.5.

## Router on vs off

A request counts as "off" when the prompt it belongs to was sent with `/router off` set.

| Router | Sessions | Requests | API-equivalent | Opus 5.5 only | Net savings |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| On | 10 | 294 | $13.73 | $19.38 | $5.64 (29.1%) |
| Off | 5 | 246 | $16.82 | $16.82 | $0.00 (0.0%) |
| **All** | 13 | 540 | $30.56 | $36.20 | $5.64 (15.6%) |

Two sessions spanned the switch and appear in both rows. The all-requests figure is lower only because the router-off stretch saves nothing by definition.

## Takeaways

- With the router on, it saved about 29% against Opus-only ($5.64 of $19.38) across 10 sessions. Most of that came from turns routed to Sonnet.
- Switching and classifying are cheap. The switch cache penalty was $1.10 (about 4% of actual cost) and the classifier cost under a cent over 48 calls.
- Haiku answered few turns: 6 requests, 1.3% of spend. With a warm cache, `/router sticky` keeps cheap picks on the current model, so the savings come mostly from Sonnet.
- The simulated `sonnet-first` policy would have saved about 35% on all requests, including the router-off stretch, but it is a simulation and says nothing about answer quality.
- The sample is small: one user, 10 routed sessions over two days, one override. Do not extrapolate from it.
